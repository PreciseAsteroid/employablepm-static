Low friction, high lock-in

Low friction, high lock-in.

Tools like Muse, Instinct, Grok, Copilot and the rest are very easy to start using. The friction is tiny. You just start.

But if you don’t take care of your context, you will find it hard to switch. Make sure the agent uses a context you can carry from vendor to vendor. Otherwise you are tied to their processes.

That context should hold information about you, and the processes themselves, the steps and so on. And what you learn from each and every iteration.

Leave that to the agent, and you don’t know what’s stored in it.
