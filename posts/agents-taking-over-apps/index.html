Agents taking over apps

Agents taking over apps

I was standing on the Paris metro, hungry, staring at Google Maps, and I could not pick a ramen place.

There were too many pins. Too many reviews to read. I kept checking which one was on my way to the hotel, and which one was actually good.

So I gave up on the map and sent Instinct one voice message. Find me ramen, on my route.

It answered at once. A place on my route, with the best reviews. One of the best bowls of ramen I have ever had.

The map knows the places. It does not know my evening. Instinct knew where I slept, where I landed, and where I was going.

The apps are not going to die because agents click faster. They are going to die because agents have better context and much easier interfaces.

When did an app last waste your evening like that?
