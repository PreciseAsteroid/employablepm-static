I've posted a few times about AI for Product Managers and how I use AI for side projects.
The numbers? Meh. Impressions, but not many likes, no comments.

But then through side talks people started DMing me and asking for more infromation...


> "Can you show me how you actually use this?"
"What are you using with your teams?"


It got me thinking that there is a gap in knowledge, especially hands-on and down to earth one. And Perhaps there is also some anxiety issue around that topic.

**Everyone talks about AI, but I am not using it beyond chats and prompts. Is something wrong with me?

**Sure, there's lots of information out there and lots of FOMOs (which I guess fuels that anxiety), but most of it is more like 👇


> "New AI tool! 🤖 Watch this 2-min demo!"


Not very helpful.

So I decided to start this blog to fill that gap. I hope it would also ignite conversations so we could all learn from each other.

I named it **Employable PM** because I truly believe that **AI isn't optional anymore**. So why not push that anxiety up to 11? 🙃


Here's are a few topics I plan to cover:


- **My AI Workflows** – Step-by-step guides to get things done faster
- **Why PMs should (stop fearing from) Code** – Hands-on software == better decisions == Better comms. What stack to use and why (hint: I use Ruby on Rails. also with this blog)
- **Building side projects with AI** – From idea to deployment
- **AI powered Analytics Flows** – SQL + AI → Insights in minutes
- **Product Discovery** – AI-powered opportunity sizing
- **Strategy in the AI Era** – Roadmaps that adapt weekly, not yearly
- **Onboarding and Managing PMs in this new world** – Train juniors to think like seniors
- **Taste > Data** – When gut feeling still wins (and how to train it)
